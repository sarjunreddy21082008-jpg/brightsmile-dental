Questions

Frequently asked

Do you accept my dental insurance?

We work with most major insurance providers and will handle the paperwork for you. Call us and we'll verify your coverage before your visit.

Is teeth whitening safe?

Yes. Our professional whitening is enamel-safe and supervised by a dentist, giving faster and safer results than over-the-counter kits.

What if I have a dental emergency?

Call us right away. We keep same-day slots free every day for pain, swelling, chipped or knocked-out teeth.

Do you offer payment plans?

Absolutely. We offer interest-free monthly plans on eligible treatments so quality care fits your budget.

How often should I visit the dentist?

Most people benefit from a check-up and clean every six months. We'll recommend a schedule that suits your needs.
